01
The Beginning
In 2013, Telugu cinema had a problem. Brilliant stories were dying in the hands of directors who couldn't find a banner willing to take a risk on them.
Kandregula Adhinarayana had seen this happen one too many times. He had watched talented writers and directors walk away from their dream projects, not because the stories weren't good enough — but because the industry gatekeepers demanded names, guarantees, and star power before they would even read a script.

So he decided to do something about it. In a modest office in Hyderabad, with a vision larger than his initial budget, SKML Motion Pictures was born. The name carried a promise: that every film under this banner would be made with full commitment, regardless of the size of the cast or the scale of the production.

Those first years were not easy. Getting distributors to trust a new banner took persistence. Convincing experienced crew to work with an emerging producer required not just money but a track record — something SKML was still building. Every decision was made carefully, every rupee invested with intention.

"The first film we produced felt like climbing a mountain with no map. But we got to the top, and the view from there changed everything."

— Kandregula Adhinarayana, Founder
By the time the first theatrical release happened, SKML had already established something more valuable than a hit film — a reputation. Directors knew that Adhinarayana would not abandon a project midway. Crew knew they would be paid on time. And slowly, the industry began to notice.

The Man Behind the Banner

Founder & Producer
He didn't come from a film family. There was no inherited studio, no godfather in the industry. What Kandregula Adhinarayana had was something rarer — an obsessive love for cinema and an entrepreneur's willingness to bet on himself when no one else would.
Growing up in Andhra Pradesh, he was the kind of person who watched films not just to be entertained, but to understand how they were made — the choices behind every shot, every dialogue, every edit. Long before he became a producer, he was studying the craft from the audience's seat.

His entry into production was not planned as a business move. It was an act of frustration — frustration at watching good stories go untold. When a close friend, a filmmaker with a gripping script, couldn't find a single producer willing to back the project, Adhinarayana stepped in. That first co-produced film didn't break records, but it taught him everything: how a production is structured, where money gets wasted, how distribution works, and most importantly — what directors actually need from a producer.

He then spent three years learning the full cycle — attending shoots, sitting in edit suites, negotiating with distributors, and understanding OTT compliance requirements before most regional producers even knew what metadata meant. When he formally launched SKML Motion Pictures as a full production house, he wasn't just a producer — he was a complete filmmaker by knowledge, even if the camera was always in someone else's hands.

Today, that depth of understanding shows in every SKML production. Budgets are optimised not by cutting corners, but by knowing exactly where every rupee creates value on screen. Directors who work with SKML consistently say the same thing: it feels like working with someone who has actually been on a film set, not just funded one.
"I never wanted to be the biggest production house. I wanted to be the most trusted one."
Outside of films, Adhinarayana is known in Hyderabad's creative circles as someone genuinely invested in the ecosystem — mentoring young directors, facilitating introductions between writers and studios, and consistently advocating for better revenue-sharing models for regional OTT content. His influence extends well beyond the films that carry the SKML name.

Behind the Camera
Where every film is made
Great cinema doesn't happen by accident. It's the result of hundreds of decisions made under pressure, on set, in the edit room, and in distribution meetings. Here's a glimpse of how SKML works.
Film set — wide shot
Director on set

SKML's production process is built around one principle: waste nothing, compromise nothing. Every project begins with a detailed breakdown of the script — scene by scene — to identify where the budget can be optimised without affecting the story's visual or emotional impact. Locations are scouted with an eye for production value. Crew is selected not just for skill but for their ability to work within realistic timelines.
Post-production at SKML is treated with the same seriousness as the shoot itself. From colour grading to sound design to VFX, each department is managed closely to ensure the final product holds up on the large-format screens of OTT platforms. The goal is always the same: when a viewer sits down to watch an SKML film on Amazon Prime or Aha, they should not be able to tell it was made on an optimised budget.

What We Stand For
The values that drive every decision we make
A production house is only as good as the principles it operates by. At SKML, these aren't values written for a website — they're decisions made every single day on set, in negotiations, and in how we treat the people who trust us with their stories.
01
Story first, always
We evaluate every project starting with one question: is this a story worth telling? Budget, star power, and genre come after. The script is the foundation of everything we build.
02
Affordable without compromise
We've proven over 12 years that optimised production costs don't mean lower quality. We find efficiencies in process, not in cutting corners that show up on screen.
03
Relationships over transactions
Every director, every crew member, every distribution partner we work with is a long-term relationship. That's why 80% of the directors who work with SKML come back for their next film.
